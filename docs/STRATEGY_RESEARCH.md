# Strategy Lab: research references and roadmap

TradeBo is a browser-based research and evaluation project. This review covers ten representative projects, not the full market of trading software or every statistical method. The comparison identifies useful engineering and evaluation practices; inclusion does not endorse a project, strategy, or expected investment outcome.

Official repositories, documentation, and license files were checked on October 22, 2026. Repository activity and a documented method are not evidence of profitability. The current [Strategy Lab contract](STRATEGY_LAB.md) defines what TradeBo actually calculates.

## Practices adopted in the current lab

The lab uses independently implemented TypeScript rules and a shared evaluation contract. It does not embed the reviewed trading engines.

- **A declared input boundary:** consecutive monthly market and cash total returns; synthetic examples remain visibly synthetic; imported third-party data stays local.
- **A declared timing boundary:** a month's target depends only on earlier completed months. This is a monthly allocation approximation, not an order-fill simulator.
- **Comparable baselines:** market, cash, and a fixed 60% market / 40% cash allocation accompany five fixed illustrative rules. Every final-window comparison starts from cash with the same capital and dates.
- **A separate selection process:** expanding training windows choose a rule for subsequent development periods. The final rule ID is chosen before the reserved final 60 months and remains fixed there; its lagged signals still update.
- **Cost accounting:** turnover uses the allocation after market and cash returns have caused it to drift. Initial investment has a cost; each adaptive selector has its own holdings ledger.
- **Bounded uncertainty language:** the paired moving-block bootstrap describes the observed return-difference series conditional on the user-inspected rule. The inspected rule is distinct from the automatic development winner. The interval does not correct for strategy selection or repeated inspection.
- **Reproducibility and tests:** manifests preserve inputs and versions; independent arithmetic checks, future-perturbation checks, and browser tests accompany the implementation.

These choices are adaptations for TradeBo's small static application. They are not a claim to reproduce any reviewed framework's full capabilities.

## Method roadmap

The current scope is eight fixed market/cash rules. The families below are organizational categories, not a complete taxonomy of trading. Proposed extensions require their own data and validation contracts before they become features.

| Family                       | Current scope or proposed extension                                                                                    | Required data and validation                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Baselines                    | **Current:** market, cash, monthly 60/40 market/cash                                                                   | Common dates, lag contract, cash initialization, cost reconciliation, and benchmark parity. The cash sleeve is not a bond index.                                                                                               |
| Trend                        | **Current:** completed market wealth index versus its trailing ten-level mean                                          | Prefix-invariant signals; exact window endpoints; common warm-up. This is a total-return-index rule, not a stock-price moving-average claim.                                                                                   |
| Momentum                     | **Current:** trailing 12-month market growth versus cash growth                                                        | Compound both sleeves over the same completed months; do not confuse time-series momentum with cross-sectional stock ranking.                                                                                                  |
| Reversal                     | **Current:** market exposure after a negative trailing three-month market return                                       | Exact sign threshold and fixed window; test positive, negative, and zero-return boundaries.                                                                                                                                    |
| Volatility                   | **Current:** trailing 12-month sample volatility scales market exposure toward a 10% annualized target, capped at 100% | Sample-SD and annualization checks, zero-variance policy, and turnover costs. The target does not guarantee realized volatility.                                                                                               |
| Breakout                     | **Current:** last completed index level exceeds all 12 preceding completed levels                                      | Exclude the comparison level from its own threshold; test ties and off-by-one errors. This is not an intraday breakout strategy.                                                                                               |
| Cross-sectional factors      | **Deferred:** value, momentum, profitability, investment, quality, and related rankings                                | Point-in-time universes and fundamentals, publication lags, delisted assets, sector/exposure controls, rebalance timing, and survivorship checks. Research factor returns alone are not an executable stock-selection dataset. |
| Pairs and relative value     | **Deferred:** spreads, hedge ratios, and mean-reversion hypotheses                                                     | Synchronized security prices, corporate actions, borrow availability/cost, hedge estimation windows, stable identifiers, and out-of-sample residual/turnover checks.                                                           |
| Portfolio optimization       | **Deferred:** minimum variance, risk budgeting, shrinkage, and hierarchical allocation                                 | Multiple investable total-return series; covariance conditioning; estimation-only windows; weight/sector/turnover constraints; comparison with equal-weight and fixed allocations.                                             |
| Regime methods               | **Deferred:** state-dependent allocation and change-point models                                                       | Causal state estimates, training-only fitting, explicit transition delays, and stability across periods. Retrospective state labels must not become historical signals.                                                        |
| Supervised ML                | **Deferred:** forecast/ranking experiments                                                                             | Timestamped features and labels, leakage-resistant preprocessing, chronological validation, overlapping-label controls, trial accounting, and an untouched evaluation policy.                                                  |
| Reinforcement learning       | **Deferred:** sequential policy research                                                                               | A validated environment, fees and trading constraints, separated training/validation/test data, multiple seeds, baseline policies, and reward-versus-evaluation reconciliation.                                                |
| Options and derivatives      | **Deferred:** volatility and payoff research                                                                           | Historical chains, expiries, corporate-action adjustments, bid/ask quotes, exercise/assignment, margin, settlement, and contract multipliers. Monthly market returns cannot supply these inputs.                               |
| Execution and microstructure | **Deferred:** order types, capacity, market impact, and broker connections                                             | Timestamped quotes/trades, exchange calendars, latency, liquidity, fills, partial fills, fees, and independent paper-trading reconciliation. A monthly allocation calculation is insufficient.                                 |

For each extension, freeze the data, signal, timing, accounting, and evaluation rules first. Preserve trial counts and negative results. An attractive curve or a best-in-sample parameter set is not enough to promote a method.

## Code licenses and data rights

The review uses public documentation as reference material. No upstream implementation is copied into Strategy Lab. TradeBo's own implementation retains the repository's license.

If upstream code is later included, its license and notices need to be preserved and assessed for the proposed integration. In particular, Backtrader and Freqtrade are GPL-3.0, backtesting.py is AGPL-3.0, and vectorbt adds a Commons Clause condition to Apache-2.0. Vectorbt must not be described as unrestricted Apache-2.0 software; the condition specifically restricts selling products or services whose value derives substantially from its functionality. [Vectorbt license](https://github.com/polakowo/vectorbt/blob/master/LICENSE.md)

A software license does not grant rights to redistribute market datasets, exchange feeds, or provider trademarks. TradeBo does not bundle the Kenneth French research files or other third-party market data. The [import contract](STRATEGY_LAB.md#kenneth-french-files-and-data-vintages) records the source definitions and the distinction between a local import and permission to redistribute a dataset.
